About

Click the map, then use arrow keys to pan, +/- to zoom and 0 to fit. Drag to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom.

Builtin shows the map sprite with the tint applied. Raw shows the sprite source unmodified. Satellite and topographic views use their own colors.

Coordinates are meters from the map center: X east, Z north. Grid cells are 10 km wide.

Detailed views load pre-rendered tiles as you zoom. Topographic contours follow the extracted ground model. Auto shows finer elevation intervals when zoomed in; Settings also offers fixed intervals and line smoothing. Zero smoothing shows the unsmoothed contours. Elevations are meters above the game's sea level.

Topographic Settings offers darker lowlands, green-to-cream elevation colors, and relief lit from the northwest. Strength adjusts their subtlety beneath the contours. These styles use ground elevations; roads and buildings remain visible, and water stays blue.
