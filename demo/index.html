About

Click the map, then use arrow keys to pan, +/- to zoom and 0 to fit. Drag to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom.

Builtin shows the map sprite with the tint applied. Raw shows the sprite source unmodified. Satellite and topographic views use their own colors.

Coordinates are meters from the map center: X east, Z north. Grid cells are 10 km wide.

Detailed views load smaller tiles as you zoom. Topographic contour lines stay crisp, with the interval adjustable in Settings. Elevations are meters above the game's sea level.
